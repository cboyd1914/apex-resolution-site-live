# Apex Resolution Brand Context

## Positioning

Apex Resolution helps HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and other home-service owners make lead follow-up and daily execution more consistent.

Core line: **Automate Consistency, Not Decisions™**

## Ideal client

- Home-service company
- $500K–$3M annual revenue
- 5–20 employees
- Missed calls, slow follow-up, unclear marketing results, or owner-dependent operations

## Client language

Use plain descriptions:

- Leads fall through the cracks
- Calls go unanswered
- Follow-up depends on who is working
- The owner has to check everything
- Staff problems consume the week
- Marketing spend is hard to trace

## Desired outcomes

- New inquiries receive a fast response
- Follow-up happens without reminders
- The owner can see where leads came from
- The team follows a repeatable process
- The owner is not the daily bottleneck

## Voice

- Warm and direct
- Short sentences
- Solution first
- Use real situations rather than theory
- Explain the next step
- Avoid inflated claims

## Claim rules

- Cite the source for factual claims.
- Mark anything not confirmed as unverified.
- Never invent client results, business facts, or revenue estimates.
- Use “a client,” never client names.

## Approved proof points

These are internal proof points supplied by Apex. Confirm the correct context before external use.

- A client generated 73 leads and paused ads because the team could not handle more.
- A client reached a 95% call answer rate, up from 45%.
- A client reached a 62% booking rate, up from 28%.
- Average monthly revenue increase per client: $8,000. This must be presented only with the supporting measurement period and client set. Until that documentation is attached, mark the claim unverified for publication.
