# Apex Offers

## Missed-Call Recovery

Price reference: $297/month.

Purpose: respond to missed calls quickly, capture the lead, and move the conversation toward booking.

Use when:
- Calls are regularly missed
- Office coverage is inconsistent
- The owner wants a low-risk starting point

Do not promise a specific booking or revenue result.

## Consistency System

Price reference: $1,697/month.

Purpose: coordinate lead response, follow-up, pipeline visibility, reporting, and team execution.

Use when:
- Leads are not followed consistently
- The owner has to chase the team
- Marketing results are unclear
- Multiple systems or people are involved

## Offer selection rule

Recommend the smallest offer that directly addresses the confirmed problem. Do not force the larger service without evidence that the client needs it.
