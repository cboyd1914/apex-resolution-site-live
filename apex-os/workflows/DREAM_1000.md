# Dream 1000 Workflow

## Trigger

A qualified HVAC, plumbing, electrical, or home-service company is added to the prospect list.

## Flow

1. **Intake** — validate company, location, website, contact, and fit.
2. **Research** — run the Research Agent and store sources.
3. **QA** — check the brief for unsupported claims and audience fit.
4. **Human approval** — Carlos approves the angle and channel.
5. **Draft outreach** — create email, LinkedIn note, call opener, and Loom outline.
6. **CRM update** — create or update the GoHighLevel contact and opportunity.
7. **Send or schedule** — only after approval.
8. **Follow-up** — use a defined cadence; stop on reply, opt-out, invalid contact, or disqualification.
9. **Learning** — record response, objection, meeting, and offer fit.

## Required fields

- Company
- Website
- Market
- Trade
- Contact name
- Contact role
- Email
- Phone
- Source URLs
- Confirmed observations
- Hypotheses
- Recommended offer
- Approval status
- Last contact
- Next action
- Outcome

## Human decisions

- Whether the prospect is qualified
- Which problem to lead with
- Whether outreach is sent
- Pricing, scope, and proposal terms

## Failure handling

- Missing source: return to research
- Conflicting company identity: stop and review
- QA failure after three revisions: route to Carlos
- Opt-out: mark do-not-contact and end workflow
